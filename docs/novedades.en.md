# What's new in Stackshot

What each version brings, told for the people who use it. Stackshot shows it inside the app (in the new-version notice,
when you update, and under Home > About > What's new) and also publishes it on each release's page.

Format: one `## X.Y.Z` section per version and, in it, one line per item: `- **Title.** Short explanation.`

## 2.2.2

- **Korean interface.** In this development build you can choose 한국어 under Settings › General › Appearance › Language. Automatic mode recognizes Windows in Korean, and the language you chose yourself is kept.

## 2.2.1

- **Saved styles.** In the editor's backdrop panel, + saves the backdrop you have (colour, margin, shadow, inset, alignment…) and "Saved styles" brings any of them back in one click.
- **Smoother backdrop sliders.** Margin, inset, shadow and corners respond instantly, on large captures too.
- **Your mascot truly rests.** If you are away for more than ten minutes it stops being drawn and spends nothing; when you come back it wakes up as always.
- **More exact scrolling captures.** With smooth scrolling, no lines are lost along the way any more.
- **All the news when you update.** If you come from an older version, you see everything new since yours, version by version.
- **Fixes.** Chopper and Kero-chan say their own lines, the section strip reaches the end with Shift+wheel, and a page never leaves a heading without its card.

## 2.2.0

- **Backdrops like CleanShot X.** In the editor, the Fondo button opens a side panel: gradients, your desktop wallpaper and your own pictures, the capture itself blurred, plain colours and any other colour, plus sliders for margin, inset (with auto-balancing), shadow and corners, and alignment and proportion.
- **Drag me.** The editor's bottom bar has a handle to carry the capture dragging to a chat, an email or another app.
- **Adjust before capturing.** An option (off by default) to move and adjust the area with a preview before capturing.
- **Sharper text.** In every window, the menus and the area picker's notice, which now paints each key in its own colour.
- **Searching an XML takes you to each match.** Enter, Shift+Enter and F3 jump through every match; filtering stays optional and the scroll bar no longer disappears on huge files.
- **No more stalls.** The app no longer freezes for an instant when saving settings, closing the editor or copying.
- **Thumbnails that step aside.** If you don't use them, they hide into the edge with only a sliver showing, with a tab saying how many there are; run the pointer along the edge and they come out one by one, like a dock. Set under General > When capturing.
- **Colour picker and ruler.** While choosing an area, press C to see the pixels with a loupe and copy their colour, or drag with Shift to measure.
- **Capture with a timer.** Choose the area, a 3, 5 or 10 second countdown runs and it captures with the menu or the notice already open.
- **Join two captures.** Drop one thumbnail onto another and they become a single image.
- **Your mascot, by the time of day.** Coffee in the morning, a nightcap at night, a party on Fridays and a flash at every capture.
- **Five more characters.** Stitch, Kirby, Grogu, Eevee and Charmander, each with its own trick.
- **Six more Pokémon.** Bulbasaur, Squirtle, Jigglypuff, Snorlax, Gengar and Mew, each with its own build. BMO, Doraemon and Totoro, redrawn more faithfully.
- **Twenty-four more characters.** More Pokémon (Psyduck, Togepi, Meowth, Piplup, Mimikyu, Lucario, Dragonite, Ditto and Mewtwo), Ghibli spirits (No-Face, Calcifer and a kodama), game icons (Yoshi, Pac-Man, the Creeper, the Among Us crewmate, Blinky and a Slime) and classic cuties (Snoopy, Hello Kitty, Baby Groot, Rilakkuma, Pusheen and Pochacco), each with its own build, eyes and mouth.
- **Classic game characters.** Pac-Man eating with Pinky, Inky and Clyde, the Space Invaders martian, Goomba, Boo, Toad, the Mushroom and the green 1-Up, the "?" block, Bob-omb, Chain Chomp, Red and the Angry Birds piglet, the Hollow Knight, Portal's companion cube, Bomberman, the Pooka, a Fall Guy, the Minecraft pig, the Metroid and Waddle Dee.
- **Tricks and lines for the classics.** Pac-Man eats the dots and runs from a frightened ghost; the ghosts turn blue; Boo hides its face (also when you bring the mouse close); Bob-omb goes "boom!" harmlessly; Chain Chomp lunges; the Creeper hisses and pops; Red is launched. Almost all of them say their own lines now and then, all have a crisper outline, and Pac-Man, Boo, Chain Chomp and the crewmate face the way they walk.
- **A truer Kirby and the crewmate down the manhole.** Kirby is a real ball in its own pink, with its eyes close together, its blush, little arms tucked in and red oval feet. Sent home, the crewmate drops into a manhole, as in the game.
- **Klei characters in their own style.** Wilson and Willow from Don't Starve Together and two Oxygen Not Included duplicants, drawn like their games: thick ink strokes, muted colours with hatched shading and a big head on a small body in its clothes. They go at the end of the character picker.
- **Watch how your video is saved.** When a recording stops, a card in the stack says it is being saved, and then the thumbnail appears in its place.
- **Dragons.** Toothless, the Light Fury, Stormfly, Hookfang and Meatlug, each with its own build and power: plasma, camouflage, spines, fire or lava.
- **Scrolling capture made easier.** The first time it shows you how it works, warns you if the page won't scroll and tells you how many screens you have taken.
- **Notes on the desktop.** Pin a note and it stays as a coloured card above everything (or not), locked if you like; tick its tasks with a click and it comes back after a restart. While writing it, Enter keeps following the list.
- **Windows that don't get cut off.** My reports, the news and the notices no longer leave the screen as they grow, and they move by dragging from any empty spot.
- **A long Markdown, as a PDF.** If a document is too long for one image, it offers to save it as a PDF.
- **Only what you use.** The mascot's menu and the day's summary only mention water, breaks or steps if you have them on.
- **Report a problem or propose an idea from the app.** Under Home > About > Report a problem you tell it without leaving Stackshot and without a GitHub account, and in "My reports" you see how each one is going and the replies.
- **Healthy breaks with your mascot.** It reminds you to drink water, rest your eyes, move and breathe, and if you like it does the break with you. Set under Mascot > Settings; it always tells you what it did with your answer.
- **Your mascot, also a helper.** From its menu it reminds you of whatever you ask ("Remind me of something…", and if the computer was off it tells you when you return), notes whatever you want in your Notas.md ("Note something down…"), and if you drop an image, a video, an XML, a Markdown or a text on it, it opens it where it belongs or writes it down. With the XML folder watched, it tells you when one arrives with a fault.
- **Health with your mascot.** A water goal with "+1 glass", a day's summary your way, breaks that adapt to what you do and, if you connect it, your Pixel Watch or Fitbit through Google Health.
- **Focus mode with your mascot.** Work blocks with breaks in between (the Pomodoro technique): the mascot reads beside you without making a sound and carries the clock on top of its head, healthy breaks wait for the rest and, if you leave, the clock stops until you return. Start from its menu ("Focus") or under Mascot > Settings.
- **Faster and smoother.** Large XMLs open sooner, and the thumbnails, the tray panel, the editor and the video player run smoother and spend less.
- **It confirms what you do.** When you copy, save, sort or insert an index, a button or a notice tells you it is done; and if something fails, it explains what happened.
- **.md and .xml files open at once.** The window appears already showing the document, with no intermediate screens.
- **Pasting right after capturing removes the thumbnail.** Also in browsers, Teams and other apps that paste very fast.
- **Two new characters: Kero and Kamina.** They are under Mascot > Character, and their pieces can be mixed with everything else.
- **The news, inside the app.** When there is a new version or you have just updated, Stackshot tells you what it brings, as it does now.
- **Fixes.** Thumbnails no longer leave their buttons showing when you hover over several in a row, the scrolling capture saves what it has if it fails halfway, and copying a capture's text lives in the round "T" button.
