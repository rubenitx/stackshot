# Changelog

## Unreleased

- **Windows' capture shortcuts, answered by Stackshot.** Put <kbd>Win</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>S</kbd> (or <kbd>Win</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>R</kbd>, <kbd>Win</kbd>+<kbd>Print Screen</kbd>, the Game Bar's <kbd>Win</kbd>+<kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>R</kbd>…) on any action and it opens Stackshot instead of the Snipping Tool, at once and without signing out; take it off and it is Windows' again. Atajos has a "Atajos de Windows" group to use Win+Shift+S, Win+Shift+R and Win+Print Screen in one click (and give them back). A shortcut field now records these combinations too: before, Windows took them before the field could. This uses a low-level keyboard hook on a thread of its own, installed only while one of those shortcuts is in use or a field is recording (issue #6).
- **Who has a busy shortcut, and what to do.** Atajos names the app that holds a combination (ShareX and Greenshot from their own settings; Lightshot, Snagit, PicPick, Snipaste, Flameshot, FastStone, Screenpresso, Gyazo, Monosnap, PowerToys, NVIDIA and AMD from their usual keys; OneDrive and Dropbox when they may take Print Screen), lets Stackshot answer it first without closing anything, or closes the app (gently, and only on a second click by force); the busy-shortcuts notice says who instead of guessing.
- **A PDF viewer in the spirit of Preview.** Pages one under the other with a sidebar of thumbnails, smooth scrolling, zoom around the pointer (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+wheel, pinch) and pages drawn pixel for pixel at every zoom, sharper pieces when zoomed far in; it remembers the page and zoom of each document. Drawn by Windows' own engine (Windows.Data.Pdf): nothing to download.
- **Highlight, underline, strike out, notes and pen on PDFs.** <kbd>H</kbd>, <kbd>U</kbd>, <kbd>K</kbd>, <kbd>N</kbd>, <kbd>P</kbd>, in six colours, with a floating bar over what is selected and undo; highlights are multiplied into the page like a real highlighter, so the words stay black. The marks are kept apart and the PDF is never touched until asked: "Exportar PDF con las marcas" writes a copy, "Guardar las marcas dentro de este PDF" adds them to the file, both as real annotations in an incremental update (the original bytes stay as they were) that any viewer shows and Acrobat edits; the result is opened again with Windows' engine before it is kept. Sticky notes already in a PDF (Acrobat's, anyone's) are shown too.
- **Search and copy the text of a PDF.** Windows does not hand out a PDF's text, so each page is read with Windows' OCR in the background (and kept for next time): search ignores case and accents, shows the matches with their words in the sidebar, and text is selected and copied like in any viewer (double click a word, triple click a line, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>A</kbd> the page). It works on scanned PDFs too.
- **Pages as captures.** Each page, with its marks, becomes a capture in the stack (one, the ones picked in the sidebar with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> or <kbd>Shift</kbd>, a range, or several in one tall image), is copied as an image, saved as PNG, opened in the editor to annotate further or printed (fitted and turned to the paper). A zone (<kbd>R</kbd>) is captured, copied (image and text), read or annotated the same way. The marks also copy as a Markdown reading summary, page by page.
- **Text, logs and data.** A text viewer for .txt, logs, code and settings files, JSON and CSV: any size (a log of hundreds of MB opens at once), encodings and line ends found from the bytes, line numbers, colours, search with a filter that keeps only matching lines, wrapping, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>G</kbd>. Logs show their levels in colour with error and warning counts (<kbd>F8</kbd> goes to the next), filter by level and follow live as they are written. JSON shows as a tree beside its formatted text, with paths to copy and the line and column of any error; CSV and TSV as a table with the header on top, sorting by column, numbers on the right and copying for Excel, separator found by itself (the semicolon of Spanish Excel too).
- **Open with Stackshot.** General > Archivos offers Stackshot for PDF, text and logs, JSON and CSV, and images (PNG, JPG and BMP go to the editor); the tray has "Abrir PDF o documento…". Files open in the Stackshot that is running.

## 2.2.1

- **Everything new since your version.** Right after an update, the news window lists what every version since the one you had brings, newest first under its number (from 2.1 straight to 2.2.1: both), and no longer stops at twenty items.
- **Saved backdrop styles.** The editor's sidebar starts with "Estilos guardados" and a <kbd>+</kbd>, as CleanShot X's Presets: <kbd>+</kbd> keeps the backdrop on screen (kind, colour, margin, inset, shadow, corners, alignment and proportion) and the menu brings any of the last eight back in one click, or removes one.
- **Backdrop sliders at full speed.** While a slider is dragged, the editor no longer paints the background and the shadow under the card that covers them, keeps the capture's rounded corners when they move less than a pixel, and stretches smooth backgrounds the fast way: on a Full HD capture a repaint while dragging Margen went from about 80 ms to under 20, and Inserción, Sombra and Esquinas stay around 12 ms. Released, it is drawn at full quality, pixel for pixel the same.
- **Scrolling captures keep their length.** With smooth scrolling (4.2 rows a frame at 125 %), the fraction of a row left over is carried to the next frame instead of lost on every one.
- **Fixes.** Chopper and Kero-chan say their own lines for those who had Reno or León; Shift+wheel reaches the last section chip; a part of a page that fails to build is left out whole, never as a caption without its card; removing one of your pictures keeps the saved styles pointing at the right ones.
- **Nothing drawn while you are away.** After ten minutes without keyboard or mouse, the napping desktop mascot and the main window's mascot stop redrawing and only check, twice a second, for your return; on a screen that never switches off this saves what the nap cost all night (about 7 % of a core). The first move wakes them as before.

Opening or dropping another video in the player keeps the current one playing meanwhile.
- **The video editor stays open after exporting.** A successful export goes to the stack with a "Exportado" note and the window stays, so the same edit can also go out as a GIF or in another size. With nothing to change, Exportar says so instead of closing the window. <kbd>Esc</kbd> cancels an export at once; the leftovers are cleaned up in the background. Work files left in the temp folder by a forced close are removed a day later.
- **FFmpeg download that can't hang.** A download that receives nothing for 30 s is given up with a clear message, there must be about 400 MB free before it starts, errors say what happened in plain words (no connection, proxy asking for a password, secure connection blocked, disk full), and "Ya lo tengo…" only accepts a file that answers as FFmpeg.
- **Safer saving of documents.** When an antivirus or OneDrive holds a Markdown or XML file for a moment, saving tries again before writing over it, and the temporary copy is kept until the new text is fully written.
- **Reports keep personal data out.** The title, description and steps of "Informar de un problema" go through the same cleaning as the log (user and computer names, profile paths, e-mail addresses, ID, IBAN, card and phone numbers). The desktop mascot no longer writes the name of the full-screen app into the log.
- **Uninstalling without a helper process.** The running program is moved aside to the temp folder (under a name Windows won't run) and the install folder is removed at once, instead of a hidden command window retrying in a loop, which security software may flag. The next install clears what was moved aside.
- **A new install starts with capturing only.** The welcome window has a "Mascota y pausas saludables" switch (off): the mascot in the main window, the healthy breaks and the health comments come only if you turn it on, and can be turned on later in their settings. Silent and MSI installs start with them off. Existing installs keep what they have.
- **The watched XML folder is generic.** General > Avanzado > "Abrir los XML nuevos de una carpeta" no longer assumes a particular program or folder: you choose the folder, or your IT department sets it with the new `XmlWatchFolder` policy (docs/it-policies.md), which is also what makes the group appear on PCs where it was never set up. If you used the default folder, choose it once in that row.

- **Healthy breaks with the mascot.** Mascota > Ajustes > Pausas saludables reminds you to drink water (every hour by default), rest your eyes with the 20-20-20 rule (every 30 min), move (every hour, squats and a back stretch in turn) and, if you like, breathe slowly for a minute. The desktop mascot asks with a small card: "Bebo un vaso contigo", "Empezar (20 s)", "Hacer 8 sentadillas" or "Respirar 1 minuto" start a guided break it does with you (a glass of water it drinks, a hand shading its eyes and a 20-second countdown, squats counted one by one, a breathing ring that grows and shrinks with "Inspira…" and "Suelta…"); "Ya lo he hecho", "Recuérdamelo en 10 min" and "Hoy no" answer it. Only time at the computer counts (5 minutes away is already a break), reminders are at least 4 minutes apart and wait during calls (microphone or camera in use), presentations, full-screen apps, recordings and Windows' quiet time. Each break done adds to today's count, shown in the settings and in "Una pausa conmigo" in its desktop menu, and is a friendship point. Without the desktop mascot, they can come as Stackshot notices (off by default). The four breaks are also new commands: Bebe agua, Descansa la vista, Estira las piernas and Respira conmigo.
- **Report a problem from the app.** "Informar de un problema" (Inicio > Acerca de) opens a form instead of GitHub: "Algo falla" or "Tengo una idea", a title, what happened and how to repeat it, and optionally the end of Stackshot's log (with the user name, computer name, profile paths, e-mail addresses and ID, Social Security, IBAN, card and phone numbers removed; "Ver lo que se envía" shows exactly what goes). Without the relay, or if it fails, the issue opens in the browser with everything filled in.

When Stackshot starts just to open a file, the document comes first and the cleanup of temporary captures, the main window after an install and the desktop mascot wait until it is on screen.
- **Pasting right after capturing removes the thumbnail.** A paste in the first second after a capture was taken for a clipboard tool, and browsers and Electron apps (Brave, Chrome, Teams, Cursor) never ask again, so the thumbnail stayed. Now a read from the app in front (or its helper process, as with Teams) counts at once; reads from clipboard history and other tools in the background still don't. A capture renamed on disk is still found when it is pasted.
- **Copy text is a round "T" button.** On the thumbnail, "Texto" moved from the middle to a round button with a T next to Editar (bottom left), with its name on hover; the middle keeps only Copiar and Guardar/Abrir.

## 2.1.0

- **Find any setting.** A "Buscar ajustes" field at the top of the main window's sidebar (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>F</kbd>, except on Historial, where it searches the history) finds every row of every section as you type, ignoring accents and case, with every word in any order and the usual synonyms ("micro" finds the microphone, "modo oscuro" finds Tema, "atajo" finds the keys). Results are grouped by section with their icon, description and place ("General › Al capturar"); the arrow keys and <kbd>Enter</kbd> open one, which scrolls to the row and makes it glow for a moment; <kbd>Esc</kbd> goes back. The index is built from the real pages, so it never falls behind them. The long General and Grabación pages start with a strip of chips, one per group, that scroll to it and stay pinned under the toolbar showing where you are.
- **Historial: find a capture by what it said.** A new Historial section lists every capture, video and GIF (temporary and saved, the dated subfolders included) by day, with thumbnails, filters (Todo, Capturas, Vídeos, GIF) and a search that looks in their names and, for images, in their text: Stackshot reads each capture with the OCR built into Windows, on the computer, in the background (paused while you capture or record), and keeps the text in a compressed index in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Stackshot\historial` (the newest 5,000 captures). Matches are highlighted in a snippet of what the capture said. General > Recientes > "Buscar por el texto de las capturas" turns the reading off and deletes the index; "Borrar el índice de texto" empties it. ## 1.4.0

- **Record sound.** The computer's sound (whatever plays: videos, calls, notifications) and/or the microphone, with a microphone picker under Grabación. While recording, the bar shows a live level for each source and a click mutes the microphone. Sound and image share one clock: measured offset 5–45 ms, with no drift however long the recording. The voice is brought to a steady level (EBU R128, one fixed gain, no pumping) and the mix never clips.
- **"Cine" quality.** Lossless capture at 60 fps in step with the monitor, saved at twice the resolution (up to 4K) so 4:2:0 video keeps every pixel's color: thin and colored text look exactly as on screen and survive re-encoding by YouTube, Teams or LinkedIn (49.6 dB PSNR against the original, versus 29.7 dB at native resolution). 320 kbps sound. Saving takes about twice the recording's length at full screen.
- **Smoother recording.** Frames now come straight from the compositor (DXGI Desktop Duplication) on the GPU instead of a GDI copy, on their own thread: every new screen image is taken and placed in its own frame, cut where the monitor's refreshes leave room. Areas larger than 1920×1200 are converted to NV12 on the GPU and encoded by the graphics card when there is one. Rotated monitors, monitors on two graphics cards or machines without duplication fall back to GDI.
- **Exact colors in every player.** Videos are converted with the BT.709 matrix and tagged as sRGB screen content (they used to be converted as BT.601 and left untagged), and the frame rate is always a standard one (60 or 59.94).
- **Editor exports keep the sound**, also when trimming or changing the speed, composite marks and backdrops at full color resolution and encode at the chosen quality.
- **Lighter, airier text everywhere.** The whole interface now draws text with unhinted, fractional glyph positions, like macOS, instead of GDI's pixel-snapped glyphs that looked heavy and cramped.
- **New character: Chopper** (One Piece), with a new reindeer species, the pink doctor's top hat with its white cross, a blue nose, shorts and a Chocolate color.
- **Mascot settings one click away.** An "Ajustes" button on the mascot's card opens its settings page (personality, greetings, seasonal costume, the desktop mascot) instead of leaving them at the bottom of the page.
- **Fix.** The setting that keeps one reusable back buffer for double-buffered windows had been disabled by mistake.
